Penny Lite Support

Penny Lite keeps track of your accounts and spending on your Mac. It never signs in to your bank, and your records stay on your Mac.

Getting started

First start your free 3-day trial: choose Data → Trial and Purchase… and click Start Free 3-Day Trial. Then download a statement file from your bank's website (CSV, OFX or QFX, US dollars) and choose File → Import Bank File, or drop the file on the Penny Lite window. Penny asks which account it belongs to the first time.

Free trial and Lifetime Unlock

Try every feature free for 3 days (72 hours). After that, an optional one-time Lifetime Unlock keeps editing and imports available. There is no subscription and no automatic charge. If you don't unlock, your records stay readable, and backup, restore and export keep working.

Restore your purchase

On a new Mac or after reinstalling, choose Data → Trial and Purchase… and click Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Back up and restore your data

Choose Data → Back Up All Data… to save one backup file, and Restore Backup… to bring it back; Penny checks the file first and keeps your previous data. Backups and exports are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private (macOS FileVault protects your disk).

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact
