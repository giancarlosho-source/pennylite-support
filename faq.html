Frequently asked questions

Does Penny Lite connect to my bank?

No. You download a statement file from your bank's website and import it. Penny Lite never asks for bank passwords.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your Mac, inside Penny Lite's own folder. Nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Choose Data → Erase All Penny Lite Data… and confirm once; it permanently deletes your accounts, transactions, categories, budgets and automatic recovery backups on this Mac. It works with or without the unlock. Backups you saved elsewhere and your Apple purchases are not affected.

How do I back up my data or move it to another Mac?

Choose Data → Back Up All Data… to save one backup file. On the other Mac, choose Data → Restore Backup… and open that file. Backups aren't encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Is it a subscription?

No. After the 3-day free trial there's one optional purchase, the Lifetime Unlock. No automatic charges.

How much does the Lifetime Unlock cost?

$49.99, a one-time purchase after the 3-day free trial. No subscription, no automatic charge.

Why can't I add an account or import yet?

Adding accounts, importing and editing start with the free 3-day trial. Choose Data → Trial and Purchase… → Start Free 3-Day Trial. If the purchase options can't load, check your internet connection and choose Try Again.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your records stay yours: viewing, export, backup, restoring a Penny Lite backup, undo and erasing your data keep working. New imports (bank statements or data from an older Penny) and edits need the Lifetime Unlock.

I bought the unlock on another Mac. How do I get it here?

Choose Data → Trial and Purchase… → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I asked a family organizer to approve my purchase. When does it unlock?

Penny Lite unlocks once your Mac receives Apple's approval. If it hasn't unlocked yet, choose Data → Trial and Purchase… → Restore Purchases. Your records stay available while you wait.

Which file formats work?

CSV, OFX and QFX statements in US dollars. For CSV files Penny asks once which column is which.

What do I need to run Penny Lite?

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Not at launch. Penny Lite requires a Mac with Apple silicon.

How do I get a refund?

App Store refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I contact support?

Email gh21.support@gmail.com. Please don't send bank statements or financial files.
