Penny Lite Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Penny Lite is made by Giancarlos Hurtado. Penny Lite does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal or financial data.

What stays on your Mac

Your accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, backups and recovery copies are stored only in Penny Lite's folder on your Mac, inside the app's macOS sandbox. Files you import, back up or export go only where you choose. Penny Lite does not encrypt backups or exports itself; macOS FileVault protects them on your disk.

No network use by Penny Lite

Penny Lite contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, makes no connections of its own, and never connects to your bank.

Purchases

The free trial and the Lifetime Unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment and Apple Account information under Apple's Privacy Policy. Penny Lite only receives Apple's signed confirmation of what you purchased, which macOS keeps on your Mac.

Your control

In Penny Lite, Data → Erase All Penny Lite Data… permanently deletes the accounts, transactions, categories, budgets and automatic recovery backups Penny Lite stored on this Mac. Backups you saved elsewhere and your Apple purchases are not affected. Deleting the app and its data folder also removes everything Penny Lite stored. Back up first if you want to keep it.

Children

Penny Lite is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
